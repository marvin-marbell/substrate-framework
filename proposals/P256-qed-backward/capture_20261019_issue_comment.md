## Working idea, progress and next focus

**There is a concrete candidate idea, not just a collection of error bounds:** a quantum substrate made of local matrix fields, quantum charge variables and links between sites. The proposal is that its coupled dynamics can supply charged spin-half matter and electromagnetic excitations, with their interaction coming from the same charge-transport rule.

The target is **a consistent physical construction, not a one-species restriction**: an excitation’s charge, motion, response to rotations and interaction must ultimately follow from the same law. Different species and bound composites are welcome outcomes. The surrounding field dressing is part of the excitation—not an unrelated particle added afterward.

This is a working hypothesis supported by several executed constructions. It is not yet a complete derivation of nature's electrons and photons, and it is not a claim that quantum mechanics has emerged from a classical fluid.

### A concrete common-root candidate — 15–16 October

The new unifying hypothesis gives the same matrix-frame building block **a three-state colour part and a colourless part**:

- **Electron-like source:** a charged excitation in the colourless part. Its opposite-charge partner is the antilepton—not the proton.
- **Composite source:** quark-like constituents in the colour part can combine three at a time into a colour-neutral, charge-one object. The internal colour field remains dynamical; this is not a neutral binding force renamed as the strong interaction.
- **Photon connection:** both sources use one electromagnetic charge rule and current. The aim is for that current to generate the transverse field response and for the field to react back on the sources.

Independent execution review now confirms a finite model with the distinct lepton and composite sources on **one occupied quantum reference with common scalar and colour-loop fields**. Their excitation-energy costs are calculated in that model, not assigned separate particle masses; they are not measured electron/proton masses. The three-plus-one split, flavours, gauging and charge assignments are specified hypotheses, not a derivation of QCD or measured charges. The charge pattern resembles familiar Pati–Salam-style constructions; the algebra itself is not a novelty claim.

The scalar, conserved-population model remains a control—not the rich pair-current answer. The latest construction results are:

Charge and actual local frame angular-momentum profiles reach other cells; spin is measured from the field generators, not assigned to primitive charge operators or inferred by multiplying density by a global spin label. An independent finite-resolution consumer also records these motions and checks the readout. Two-way carrier–field responses are recorded, but weak feedback is not established as numerically converged: time-step and state-approximation checks remain substantial on its scale. Field-cut, preparation and metric assumptions remain explicit; this is not a free-electron or photon identification.

Matter and field exchange finite-lattice momentum, and additional sea excitations are populated rather than excluded. The signed current and both orders of the current/field vertex were actually calculated—not inferred solely from density changes. Evolving-neutral and frozen-feedback controls, initial field population and later changes are kept separate. Fresh independent calculations now confirm the leading same-law recoil/current response in both transverse channels, with the sea’s quantum contribution retained; they do not check the longer-time numerical magnitudes. Earlier smaller-prototype reviews already found genuine two-channel coupling after disentangling pulse-preparation effects. The new result is not yet free-space scattering or a join with the separate field-origin-spin construction.

Its coupled variational waves have run, but are not controlled full-Hamiltonian photons. These are conditional finite-model constructions, with substantive field-regulator and state-approximation limits. They are not measured electron/proton masses, an identified nature photon, or a completed derivation. The different prototypes must ultimately be built into **one chosen law and state**, not joined by combining successful plots.

The working carrier picture is a **moving charged angular excitation with quantum dressing, possible field wakes and additional excitations**. The matrix amplitude is not a particle-position observable; identical mean profiles are not the definition of success. Current outside a simple particle/field subspace is kept as a clue to additional field, sea and internal-motion components—not forced into one mode or automatically called a defect. The immediate construction now gives different spatial cells their own quantum frames and retains the incoming/outgoing field profiles. It must show local angular-momentum and charge transport together with field feedback in the same chosen law. The frames and their matrix amplitudes remain dynamical quantum degrees, not frozen backgrounds. Global theorems and sharper bounds are tools for improving working mechanisms—not permission to explore them.

### The proposed path backward

Start with the rich interacting description: charged particles and their surrounding fields, photon polarization, spin-dependent scattering, virtual electron–positron pairs, recoil and charge conservation. Ask what deeper dynamics can supply those relationships together. Do not strip them out merely to obtain a simpler model.

- **Electron branch:** the candidate uses a filled quantum matter sea coupled to fluctuating matrix fields. The field energy favours a four-matrix structure that supports Dirac-like propagation. Primitive quantum charge operators can reproduce fermionic exchange and the charged current. The next question is how the charged excitation, its spin, its mass scale and its electromagnetic dressing behave together when the fields remain dynamical.
- **Photon branch:** electromagnetic links couple to that same matter current. In the induced-response construction, the charged sea supplies magnetic stiffness and photon normalization rather than receiving a ready-made Maxwell magnetic term. The next question is whether the full coupled response supports propagating transverse quantum excitations with the required two polarizations and consistent speeds—not merely a positive static coefficient.
- **Interaction branch:** hopping through the links determines the current and two-photon contact together. Build from the current-dressed recoil candidate toward identifiable incoming/outgoing radiation states and a genuine two-leg, spin- and polarization-dependent scattering response in one consistent law. Do not relabel equal-time current insertions or changing field populations as a scattering event. Keep the frequency and momentum dependence, virtual pair channels and matter–field feedback before further coarse approximations.
- **Proton branch:** the reference is a bound quark–gluon composite with spin, electromagnetic structure and strong interactions—not an electron with the opposite charge. The local-deformation/colour-flux construction now supplies a finite moving-composite candidate. Its open questions are robustness beyond the chosen state and field ansatz, physically allowed breakup, and electromagnetic structure in a consistent law. A constrained separation cost does not establish asymptotic binding, and the present electromagnetic field is only an external probe. SU(3) gauging, constituent charges and primitive spin remain model inputs, not a derivation of QCD or nature’s proton.

These suppliers must be connected where the physics requires it; they need not be forced into one microscopic root at the outset.

### What already seems to work

- **Primitive quantum charge operators reproduce the filled-sea Compton calculation** on the selected finite inputs, including the virtual pair contributions. Removing those contributions changes the scattering result. This is more than reproducing an exchange sign, but it still uses a specified matter law.
- **Joined matter/photon/scattering constructions have been executed.** One selected-field construction returns its own photon energies and normalization to recoil and the scattering rate. A separate construction has a checked, conditional finite-time charged-carrier/photon/current join, including physical rotation and current protocols on the same prepared-state family. These are working model relationships, not just planned interfaces; that prepared state is not the newly characterized minimizing state of another branch.
- **The induced-photon branch produces positive electromagnetic response without the bare Maxwell magnetic term.** Its one-light-plus-63-heavy-species example still has about an 8% photon/matter speed mismatch. That is an unresolved physical discrepancy, not something to hide with a tuned label.
- **The full-field work now supports the candidate background rather than simply assuming it.** Normalized quantum trial states and energy bounds retain the field motion and constrain the matrix structure in actual minimizing states. New geometry and correlated-source constructions extend that connection; their final integration remains unfinished.

These are related but distinct models and parameter regimes. Their strongest results cannot be combined by silently changing the law or borrowing one model's state for another.

### Change in research focus

The next work will lead with the **mechanism and the joined physical picture**, not a queue of ever-smaller error bounds:

- Keep the rich electron, photon and interaction branches connected to the same chosen construction and state. Draw and calculate the missing feedback, rather than replace it with independent free particles or frozen fields.
- Choose calculations that can change the candidate mechanism: charged propagation with field dressing, the full electromagnetic response, and their return to spin- and polarization-resolved scattering. The first coupled object to build is the charged propagator, photon response and current vertex from one chosen law and state, with feedback retained. Reduce to a simpler description only when the relationships we need survive that reduction.
- Use local proofs and corrections when they decide one of those physical questions. Do not require every branch to be fully proved before exploring its coupled continuation. Hypotheses and partial constructions are legitimate working objects; evidence determines what can be claimed as established.
- Map the proton's composite branch at the same level of detail first. Existing neutral composite trial seeds are not a proton mechanism, and their failed stationarity checks are not evidence that the entire branch is impossible.

**Limits remain, but they are not permission gates.** Some current guarantees use enormous lattice sizes and extreme chosen parameters. They support conditional model claims, not measured mass/charge or new experimental predictions. The latest proof and execution artifacts remain in the research worktree. The issue remains open, and the aim is still the interacting physical sector—not completion of a bookkeeping checklist.
