## 1. Domain, metric and exact Hessian

Let the real inner product on traceless Hermitian four-by-four matrices be
`<A,B>=tr(AB)/4`. An admissible ordered four-frame has coordinate matrix
`X in R^(15x4)` with `X^T X=I`; this is `V_4(R^15)`. There are 60 coordinates
and **10 independent symmetric Gram constraints**, so the tangent dimension is
50. Independence is not an assumption: the constraint differential sends
`T=X S/2` to any prescribed symmetric matrix `S`. The executable constructs
its tangent basis as the null space of precisely those ten linear constraints,
not by sampling a proposed collection of deformations.

Use the coefficients `x(p)` of sea selection, positive weights `w_p`, and
`R_p=|x(p)|>0`. At a selected Clifford frame `C`, `h_0(p)=x_mu(p) C_mu` has
spectrum `(-R_p,-R_p,R_p,R_p)`. Along any smooth admissible path write
`V=dot h(0)`, `W=ddot h(0)`. The trace-Gram constraint gives the **acceleration
identity**

\[
 \operatorname{tr}(h_0 W)=-\operatorname{tr}(V^2),\qquad
 \operatorname{tr}V=\operatorname{tr}W=0.
\]

The two occupied energies need not individually be differentiable under
splitting, but their sum is analytic across the isolated occupied cluster.
With `P_±=(I±h_0/R)/2`, its second derivative is

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 e''(0)&=\operatorname{tr}(P_-W)
 -R^{-1}\operatorname{tr}(P_- V P_+ V)\\
 &=\frac{\|P_-VP_-\|_F^2+\|P_+VP_+\|_F^2}{2R}
 =\frac{\|\{h_0,V\}\|_F^2}{8R^3}.
 \end{aligned}
\]

Thus for **every** admissible tangent `T`, with `delta h_p=x_mu delta A_mu`,

\[
 H[T,T]=\mathcal E''(0)
 =\sum_p\frac{w_p}{8R_p^3}\|\{h_0(p),\delta h(p)\}\|_F^2. \tag{1}
\]

The path's acceleration disappears only because the exact budget supplies it.
Holding `W=0` in an unconstrained matrix perturbation would give the wrong
answer. First derivatives vanish by `tr(h_0 V)=0`; the witness is stationary
on the full Stiefel manifold. Formula (1) is the actual occupied-energy
Hessian, not the Hessian of an added algebra penalty.

## 2. Kernel: infinitesimal Clifford frames are inner derivations

Assume the ten-product design `Q[p,(mu,nu)]=x_mu x_nu`, `mu<=nu`, has rank ten.
The previous ten-momentum determinant witness proves this for the actual
27-point periodic `L=3` grid, including the improved band, for `a,m,v>0,r>=0`.
Since all weights are positive, (1) vanishes exactly when every
`{h_0(p),delta h(p)}` vanishes. Full design rank then implies

\[
 \{C_\mu,\delta A_\mu\}=0,\qquad
 \{C_\mu,\delta A_\nu\}+\{C_\nu,\delta A_\mu\}=0\quad(\mu\ne\nu). \tag{2}
\]

These are the derivatives of **all** Clifford relations. They define a
complex-linear derivation `D` on the complex Clifford algebra by
`D(C_mu)=delta A_mu` and the Leibniz rule: differentiating every defining
relation gives zero, so the operation descends from the free algebra to its
quotient. The selected four-generator algebra is `Mat_4(C)`; its sixteen
Clifford monomials span that algebra. No larger representation is introduced.

For completeness, innerness is constructive. In ordinary matrix units put

\[
 K=\sum_{i=1}^4 D(e_{i1})e_{1i}.
\]

Differentiating `e_kl e_i1=delta_li e_k1` and summing after multiplication by
`e_1i` gives `[K,e_kl]=D(e_kl)`. Hence `D=[K,·]` on all matrices. Since the
four inputs and variations are Hermitian, `D` respects adjoints. Consequently
`K+K^dagger` commutes with every matrix and is scalar. Remove its scalar part
and the remaining scalar imaginary part to obtain `K=iG` with `G` traceless
Hermitian. Thus every zero mode is

\[
 \delta A_\mu=i[G,C_\mu]. \tag{3}
\]

Conversely these tangents are generated by the exactly flat path
`A_mu(t)=exp(itG) C_mu exp(-itG)`. The map (3) is injective on the
15-dimensional real space of traceless Hermitian `G`: a common commutant of
the four `C_mu` is scalar, because they generate `Mat_4(C)`. Therefore

\[
 \dim\ker H=15,\qquad \operatorname{rank}H=50-15=35.
\]

The **labelled-frame** stabilizer in `U(4)` is precisely its scalar `U(1)`;
the orbit is `PU(4)`, an embedded compact 15-manifold. There is no additional
chirality, independent spin-basis, or discrete component of minimizing frames:
`Cl_4(C)` has one four-dimensional irreducible representation, and its
Hermitian representations are unitarily equivalent. Changing the orientation
of the four labelled generators is also implemented in this same orbit.
Repeated positive Hessian eigenvalues from cubic symmetry are not extra flat
modes.

The executable independently builds the orbit basis from **all 15 actual
matrix-unit commutators**, verifies its rank and tangent inclusion, and

## 3. Chart, local Morse–Bott theorem and an explicit distance tube

Use the coordinate metric

\[
 \|\delta A\|_{\rm HS}^2=\tfrac14\sum_\mu
 \operatorname{tr}(\delta A_\mu^2)=\|\delta X\|_F^2.
\]

Let `O` be the Clifford orbit and `N_C` the orthogonal complement of its
tangent **inside the Stiefel tangent**. The quotient coercivity constant is

\[
 \lambda=\min_{T\in N_C,\ \|T\|_F=1}H[T,T]>0. \tag{4}
\]

This is an exact finite matrix definition, not a fitted curvature. Unitary
invariance makes the same `lambda` work at every point of `O`.

A proper local chart is provided by the polar retraction

\[
 \mathcal R_X(T)=(X+T)\big((X+T)^T(X+T)\big)^{-1/2}.
\]

For tangent `T`, the Gram factor is `I+T^T T`, so this map is smooth,
admissible and has derivative the identity at zero. Together with local
`PU(4)` coordinates, `(U,T) -> U mathcal R_C(T) U^dagger`, `T in N_C`, has
invertible differential. The inverse function theorem therefore supplies a
local orbit-normal chart; compactness supplies a tube around the full orbit.
Inside a sufficiently small tube the occupied/empty clusters stay isolated,
and the functional is analytic. Its critical minimum manifold is exactly `O`
by the previous global selection theorem, and its normal Hessian is positive
by (4). This establishes the local Morse–Bott conclusion rather than assuming
it from an observed spectrum.

A more explicit conservative estimate avoids any unknown third-derivative
constant or numerical orbit alignment. Define

\[
 K=\sqrt{2\sum_p w_p R_p},\qquad
 \rho=\min\left\{\frac14,
 \frac{\sqrt\lambda}{\sqrt\lambda+3K}\right\}.
\]

For an admissible `X`, let `d=dist_F(X,O)` in the **ambient chord metric**. A
nearest orbit point `C` exists by compactness. Set `Delta=X-C`, and decompose

\[
 N=-\tfrac12 C\Delta^T\Delta,\quad T=\Delta-N.
\]

The Gram identity shows `T` lies in the Stiefel tangent. Nearest-point
stationarity gives `Delta perp T_C O`; `N` is normal to the entire Stiefel
tangent, so `T in N_C`. Moreover

\[
 \|N\|_F\le d^2/2,\qquad \|T\|_F\ge d-d^2/2.
\]

Use the weighted matrix norm
`||B||_*^2=sum_p w_p ||B_p||_F^2/(8R_p^3)`, and the linear map
`L(Delta)_p={h_C(p),delta h_p}`. With
`B_p=h_X(p)^2-R_p^2 I`, its exact expansion is
`B=L(T)+L(N)+(delta h)^2`. Cauchy–Schwarz gives
`||delta h_p||_F<=2R_p d`, and hence

\[
 \|L(N)\|_*\le K\|N\|_F,\qquad
 \|(\delta h)^2\|_*\le Kd^2.
\]

It follows that

\[
 \|B\|_*\ge\sqrt\lambda d
 -\tfrac12(\sqrt\lambda+3K)d^2
 \ge\tfrac12\sqrt\lambda d\quad(d\le\rho). \tag{5}
\]

The exact four-level energy identity of sea selection gives, at every
admissible frame, `||B_p||_F^2<=4R_p^2 epsilon_p^2+16R_p^3 epsilon_p`.
Since ordered traceless eigenvalues imply `0<=epsilon_p<=2R_p`,
`||B||_*^2<=3 sum_p w_p epsilon_p`. Combining with (5) proves the concrete
local error bound

\[
 \boxed{d\le\rho\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 d^2\le\frac{12}{\lambda}(\mathcal E[X]-\mathcal E_{\min}).} \tag{6}
\]

This is **not a global distance assertion**. It does not presume all
low-energy states are in this tube, nor equate an unaligned distance to `C`
with the distance to its orbit. In the tube,
`||delta h_p||_op<=2R_p d<=R_p/2`; Weyl's bound keeps two negative and two
positive eigenvalues and an occupied/empty gap at least `R_p`. Equations
(4)–(6) define finite explicit constants for any supplied weights/design.
The CLI prints numerical evaluations of those exact constants, not
interval-arithmetic-certified decimal lower bounds. Its experiment has all

Together with (6), these are `O(sqrt(E-Emin))` local operator controls.

\[

\[
