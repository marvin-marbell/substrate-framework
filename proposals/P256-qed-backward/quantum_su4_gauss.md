Its exact finite-dimensional compression uses Haar representation theory and actual raw Pauli-code matrix elements. The parent executed both complete default-polarity hierarchies; numerical observations below retain their finite selected-orbit domain.

Light L has total number 3, 4, or 5, and electromagnetic charge `q = NL - 4`. Local numbers are dynamical. Sixteen matter modes are arranged as LH, LL, RH, RL, four modes each; the existing `PauliCode` is used with its actual `S=+` two-word states and unchanged parity marker at mode 16. The marker is not a charged scalar reference or a new matter flavour.

Their simultaneous orbit is SU(4)/Z4, not the conjugacy orbit of only one adjoint matrix. The rank-15 orbit check rejects collapsed selected frames. Fixing radial/shape coordinates is an **EFT hypothesis**, not a proof of a full 60-coordinate quantum field theory.

Higgs alignment is explicitly **J=0**. No compressed-link product is used to fabricate an alignment term. ## Full local Lie Gauss reduction, not matter singlets

Let the orientation variables be `gL,gR` in SU(4)/Z4 and let `U` be the SU(4) link. At the right endpoint attach boundary representation B: trivial for q=0; fundamental 4 for q=-1; antifundamental bar4 for q=+1. Its centre charge is `b=-q mod 4`. It also carries outward EM flux q, but is not a dynamical heavy orbital.

Use the ordinary second-quantized matter representation, generated by

`Q_x^a = sum_flavours sum_ij c†_(x,f,i) (T^a)_ij c_(x,f,j)`.

The c-bilinear notation describes the matrix-unit law; the consumer builds every off-diagonal entry with `PauliCode.transfer`, hence `PauliCode.element` on the actual raw code states. No exterior/wedge sign table builds the matter Hamiltonian or generators.

For a unitary-gauge fibre function `psi(u)`, define

`Psi(gL,gR,U) = Dmatter(gL,gR) Dboundary(gR) psi(gL† U gR)`.

Under arbitrary local `hL,hR`, the argument `u=gL† U gR` is unchanged when `(gL,gR,U)` transforms to `(hL gL,hR gR,hL U hR†)`. The remaining factors transform by the full matter/boundary representation. Differentiating for every traceless Hermitian generator proves all fifteen local Lie Gauss equations at each cell; the statement is not just a centre check. Conversely any equivariant function is determined by its value at `gL=gR=1`, giving this formula. Haar invariance makes this reduction isometric after normalized orientation integration. Therefore the physical Hilbert space is the centre-restricted

`Hmatter(NH=4,NL) tensor L²(SU4) tensor HB`,

**not** a matter-singlet subspace. The orientation variables have supplied the local gauge frame and their momenta carry the non-Abelian Gauss charge.

Single-valuedness under the representatives `(gL zL,gR zR)` yields

`n_r = Ntotal_left mod 4`,

`-n_r = Ntotal_right + b mod 4`.

These follow because a matrix coefficient transforms by `zL^(-n_r) zR^(n_r)` in `gL† U gR`, while the matter/boundary factors contribute `zL^(Nleft) zR^(Nright+b)`. They are residual discrete restrictions **after** full Lie Gauss reduction. With total matter number `8+q`, omitting the charged boundary leaves no consistent simultaneous restrictions for q=±1. Two half-seas have centre charge zero in the neutral total sector; that is necessary, not sufficient by itself to solve local Lie Gauss.

### Derivative signs and orientation inertia

Take normalized fundamental generators `Tr(Ta Tb)=delta_ab/2`. In the Peter-Weyl coefficient convention below,

`Eleft^a = (Ta_r)^T tensor 1`,

`Eright^a = 1 tensor (-Ta_r)`.

These are function-coefficient operators, both with the **negative** structure-constant convention `[Ea,Eb]=-i f_abc Ec`; matter Q has `[Qa,Qb]=+i f_abc Qc`. This distinction is essential, not a changed sign in the matter representation. Right multiplication of an orientation by `exp(i t Ta)` acts on the matter factor by Q. It acts on the reduced link argument by `exp(-i t Ta) u` at L and `u exp(i t Ta)` at R. Thus the orientation derivatives are `QL-Eleft` and `QR+Qboundary-Eright`. Their invariant metric kinetic energy is exactly

`sum_a [(Eleft^a-QL^a)^2 + (Eright^a-QR^a-Qboundary^a)^2] / (2 I)`.

The consumer expands these squares, retaining the `+2 QR Qboundary` cross term as well as both link/matter cross terms. Finite positive I is exposed. There is no assumption that matter alone is neutral under each Ta, nor that reduced-unitary-gauge H must commute with the unreduced orientation generators on its fibre.

Fundamental Ta come from all fifteen primitive Hermitian matrix-unit directions. Adjoint generator components are `2 Tr(Tb [Ta,Tc])`. No preassembled spin generators are supplied.

Let `C_(i,c),a` embed irrep r in `4 tensor s`. Exact multiplication is

`<r,a,b | U_ij | s,c,d> = sqrt(d_s/d_r) C_(i,c),a conjugate(C_(j,d),b)`.

The retained maps and normalized embeddings are:

- `1 -> 4`: identity;

These exhaust nonzero fundamental multiplication maps within the full domain. Since the frozen one-body Hamiltonian is traceless, the sum of its upper-half energies is minus the lower-half sum. The stored frame is still the declared single-source `uniform_data` orbit, not a new solution of the interacting Gauss ground-state functional. ## Peierls source, Ward identity and adverse cases

Only the light hop receives `exp(i A)`. The same-H current is its analytic first A derivative and the contact its second derivative. The CLI compares both actions to central finite differences. For `W=exp(i theta nL_left)`, the exact source covariance is

`H(A+theta) = W H(A) W†`.

Every real physical block is used to check this operator identity on a reproducible vector. No full-resolvent response is computed, so no resolvent Ward or optical amplitude is claimed. Charged sectors, actual spectra and the Lie-Gauss reduction supply the other consumer evidence.

Adverse checks compute nonzero defects from **actual wrong operators**: reversed link/matter inertia cross signs, erroneously including heavy occupation in U1 electric energy, and treating the compressed link as unitary. Charged missing-boundary rejection is counted from the actual centre-filtered matter fibres. The physical Hamiltonian's Hermiticity and actual primitive Q Lie algebra are independently checked. The source covariance probe is not a substitute for the analytic full local Lie reduction.
