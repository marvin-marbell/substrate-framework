# P253/0165: integrable helical Euler sector, far-label escape, and exact linear packet shear

Status: author-derived **exploratory classical result**, not independently reviewed or promoted. Parent issue #203 remains open. This attempt deliberately uses the distinct exact `e=0` Euler background `U_0=(sin z,cos z,0)`; do not transfer its calculations to the `e=1/10` indexed construction of P253/0164.

[derivation.md](derivation.md) constructs actual smooth solenoidal finite-initial-excess states with an arbitrary signed `1/r` far label `Q`. A family at fixed nonzero `Q` has energy tending to zero as its core spreads, so that far label and Euler excess do not select a finite core scale. An explicit compact perturbation makes the naive line-integrated candidate electric field infinite-energy. A second **compact, initially zero-column** perturbation has a nonzero Euler column derivative despite zero linearized pressure, so restricting the initial column is not an invariant repair. Finally, an exact finite-energy, full-pressure linearized Euler sector has conserved norm and continuously distributed frequencies, while the centered transverse width of a compact packet grows quadratically.

The two-copy physical kinetic cross energy of these same `Q` dresses has leading coefficient `2πρQ₁Q₂(1-n_z²)/R` at fixed core widths. It is nonzero at horizontal separation but has **no** `1/R` term at vertical separation by a period of the same background, unlike an isotropic Coulomb energy. [verify_qtail_interaction.py](verify_qtail_interaction.py) checks the finite-core Plummer-tail Fourier overlap; this is not an evolved force or an exclusion of all possible collective electromagnetic maps.

Separate construction probe [carrier-probe.md](carrier-probe.md) checks the next obvious rescue: the exact pressure-free **linear** horizontal sector cannot be promoted to a nonlinear finite-energy invariant sector. Every nonzero compact horizontal solenoidal perturbation produces a nonzero quadratic vertical pressure acceleration somewhere, by a strict transverse moment identity. A compact swirl on the fixed-`Q` dress still leaves that far label and fixed-`Q` scale escape intact. This is an exact initial-time Euler pressure obstruction to one ansatz, not a general three-component carrier impossibility proof; no revision-bound independent review is claimed.
