# Time-sliced TypeSafe research-policy review

## Result

This extension turns the semantic research compass into a provisional
research-state and fixed-policy evaluation system. It reconstructs P253 as a
sequence of states, actions, outcomes, and reusable transitions. The principal
objects are:

```text
state s_t
  = frozen objective + authority + completed prior episodes
    + exact obstruction + live constructions + known failures

action a_t
  = source transfer + research operation + missing bridge

observed outcome
  = useful construction | exposing failure | repair
    | conditional progress | inconclusive exploration | process transition

scientific reward
  = closure progress + information gain + future reuse
    - scope conflict - repeated local variation
```

TypeSafe supplies typed estimates of the semantic quantities. Deterministic
code owns Git chronology, eligibility, authority boundaries, hashes,
aggregation, foundation-change licensing, and the separation between
scientific and process value. Proofs, calculations, tests, and review remain
the actual reward oracle.

The completed graph contains:

- 168 committed historical attempt episodes;
- 168 time slices with exact eligible-state hashes;
- 28 decision checkpoints distributed across the campaign;
- 159 historical counterfactual source/action edges;
- 20 cleaner historical missed-opportunity leads;
- 30 additional hindsight-sensitive leads kept separately;
- nine repeated-failure families;
- learned priors for 12 research operations;
- 65 current action candidates over five active obligations; and
- five exact-failure stuck interventions.

The generating `0162-typesafe-frontier-review` attempt is excluded from its own
historical experience buffer so the snapshot cannot score itself.

## Present capability and missing self-improvement loop

This snapshot does not yet improve its own policy. It evaluates prior and
candidate actions under a fixed semantic schema, fixed research-operation
vocabulary, fixed aggregation formula, and fixed governance controls. Its
empirical-Bayes operation priors are retrospective estimates over the typed
episode history. They inform action ranking but do not alter the policy
generator or establish that a changed policy performs better.

Recursive policy improvement requires versioned comparisons. A future loop
would preserve prior policy `pi_(t-1)` and current policy `pi_t` with the
trajectories each produced; compare their decisions and outcomes on common
time-sliced states; use that evidence to construct and freeze candidate
`pi_(t+1)`; deploy it on new obligations; and evaluate its independent outcomes
against the earlier policies before constructing `pi_(t+2)`. The mutation
operators, counterfactual design, prospective reward oracle, and promotion
criteria remain open design work. No skill or automated workflow is proposed
by this attempt.

## Chronology and hindsight control

Each attempt is represented as one episode with two times:

- `decision_at`: the first commit touching the attempt;
- `available_at`: the last commit touching the completed episode.

A source is eligible for a historical counterfactual only when
`source.available_at < target.decision_at`. The eligible identifier set is
hashed at every one of the 168 time slices.

The first visible commit is not always a clean preregistration. Thirty
episodes have a comparatively clean multi-commit decision snapshot. Seventy-
four first snapshots already contain outcome language, and 64 attempts were
committed as one result-bearing episode. The graph therefore uses two labels:

- `missed_opportunity`: high-value unconsumed transfer with structural
  correspondence at a cleaner time slice;
- `hindsight_sensitive_lead`: useful future transfer whose historical audit
  is contaminated by outcome exposure.

Neither label asserts that the counterfactual route would have succeeded.

## Five policy products

### 1. Historical decision audits

Every episode has a typed obligation, action distribution, outcome, evidence
kind, policy verdict, failure family, information score, closure score, reuse
probability, scope-conflict probability, and scientific utility. Twenty-eight
distributed checkpoints also compare the chosen action with time-eligible
prior episodes.

The clearest actionable historical finding is at attempt `0030`. Its same-
field restoring-dynamics design did not consume the already completed `0020`
independent Hessian review or the `0010` intrinsic-swirl/action carrier work.
The audit ranks:

- `0020 -> 0030`: invert the accessible mixed-Hessian failure into the full
  dynamics/action construction rather than asking an indefinite form to prove
  instability;
- `0010 -> 0030`: compose the actual finite-spin carrier/action supplier with
  the stability construction; and
- `0002/0006/0012 -> 0030`: import the exact carrier, autonomous deficit, and
  continuation requirements before selecting the restoring theorem.

This does not show that `0030` was wrong. It identifies prior information that
would have made the approach selection more complete at the time.

Another strong lead is `0044 -> 0087`: the radiation-gate analysis could have
used the earlier curved-ring Feshbach construction as the stability and
operator-closure comparator. Because `0044` itself remained conditional, this
is a design transfer, not a missing proved prerequisite.

stability class, and observation map should enter the initial action card.

### 4. Learned priors over research operations

The soft empirical-Bayes priors are learned from TypeSafe-typed historical
outcomes, weighted by information gain, closure progress, reuse, scope
conflict, and repeated-local-variant risk:

| research operation | posterior useful-transition mean | effective mass |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| direct construction | 0.612 | 8.8 |
| failure inversion | 0.595 | 19.7 |
| review and correction | 0.570 | 19.5 |
| oracle/sensitivity design | 0.566 | 12.8 |
| decomposition/factorization | 0.546 | 3.6 |
| composition with explicit glue | 0.546 | 6.7 |
| controlled limit/asymptotic | 0.531 | 3.3 |
| representation change | 0.512 | 3.6 |
| source/authority audit | 0.511 | 8.4 |
| inverse construction | 0.508 | 1.0 |
| assumption/scope repair | 0.487 | 31.7 |
| coordination/recording | 0.063 | 49.0 |

These are policy priors, not universal success rates. Their most credible
signal is comparative: direct construction, exact failure inversion,
substantive review, and exposing-oracle design repeatedly produced useful
transitions, while coordination volume supplied provenance without scientific
closure. The low effective mass for inverse construction means its estimate is
uncertain rather than disfavored.

### 5. Exact-failure stuck intervention

The system profiles each exact failure as its own obstruction and retrieves
historical sources specifically for that mechanism:

- **autonomous reset:** use `0090` to turn outgoing flux and finite-gate
  leakage into an applicability theorem for an open capture environment;
  use `0075` for an observation oracle and `0120-cipher-m2b1` for a controlled
  physical-scope upgrade;
- **continuous action scale:** use `0005` to invert Euler similarity against
  the KKS period lattice, `0003` to preserve the actual dynamics/action map,
  and `0043` to test whether the proposed state construction still imports an
  action unit;
- **false Casimir:** use `0006`'s qualitative deficit route when no Hessian gap
  is licensed, and `0030`'s full-generator route when the variational form is
  indefinite;
- **non-isolated `F'(zeta)=0` level:** use `0030`'s explicit operator/variable
  map and `0006`'s continuation/stability discipline at the actual critical
  carrier;
- **regular-trace layer:** use the same full operator map plus the physical-
  scope and applicability machinery from `0120-cipher-m2b1`, `0006`, and
  `0012` instead of varying the forbidden regular-core ansatz.

The stuck intervention requires the exact failure statement. A generic “the
campaign is stuck” state remains an unsuitable policy input.

## Corrections made during execution

Five method failures were found and repaired:

1. Historical pair values initially omitted dominant capability and operation
   support, making them incomparable with compass edges. Both now use the same
   factors.
2. Process episodes could receive scientific reward from repository utility.
   Process value and scientific utility are now separated deterministically.
3. Result-bearing first commits produced false retrospective confidence.
   Cleaner misses and hindsight-sensitive leads are now separate products.
4. Compact references such as `reviewed0005` were not recognized, causing
   consumed sources to appear missed. Four-digit attempt aliases are now
   parsed directly.
5. Global top-k ranking could erase an evidence class, and a relevant but
   unlicensed foundation extension could dominate the charge route. Channel
   anchors and explicit owner-authorization penalties now preserve diversity
   and governance.

## Reuse and feedback

An unchanged rerun makes no TypeSafe calls. A new campaign should supply its
proposal path, attempt history, accepted-authority snapshot, and exact current
obstruction cards. Future reviewed outcomes should replace semantic labels in
the operation-prior update. The most valuable feedback fields are whether a
transfer was actually consumed, what construction it changed, whether its
bridge was proved, and which downstream consumer became reachable.

The executed snapshot used TypeSafe model `jev-1.13.0`, 4,952,243 input
tokens, and 334,728 output tokens. The verifier checks all time slices and
counterfactual chronology, calibration controls, channel coverage,
foundation-change licensing, credential exclusion, and all five policy
products.
