# TypeSafe as a semantic research compass

## Result

The highest-value role found in this experiment is a **cached semantic
research layer between repository search and mathematical construction**.
TypeSafe should not choose the easiest task, choose a campaign, or act as a
physics oracle. It should make the framework's accumulated work available to
the next act of reasoning.

Each accepted claim, current provisional code module, failed mechanism, and
candidate construction is encoded once as a probabilistic vector of:

- physical and mathematical capabilities it actually supplies;
- research operations its construction can teach elsewhere; and
- scope or failure boundaries that a future route can use.

For a concrete obstruction, TypeSafe then supplies three typed judgments:

1. which capabilities and research moves the obstruction most strongly calls
   for;
2. which prior sources may transfer as a direct ingredient, construction,
   analogy, failure inversion, falsifier, conflict, or irrelevant hit; and
3. what bridge is missing and what operation the research agent should try.

The research agent reads the exact selected sources, derives concrete
candidates, and subjects them to the framework's analytic, symbolic, formal,
or numerical oracles. TypeSafe's output remains provisional context and never
changes claim authority.

This gives TypeSafe a useful role at every stage:

- **opening:** retrieve unrecognized constructions and cross-sector analogies;
- **approach design:** select a representation change, inverse construction,
  controlled limit, decomposition, composition, scope repair, oracle, or
  failure inversion;
- **stuck intervention:** contrast the exact failed mechanism with earlier
  failures and repairs;
- **review:** expose imported premises, weak structural correspondence, and
  missing applicability maps;
- **continuity:** store the resulting useful or failed transfer edge so later
  campaigns inherit it.

## Why the decomposition matters

TypeSafe returns focused typed judgments, not generated derivations. The
intelligent composition is therefore:

```text
authoritative repository facts + explicitly provisional current code
        |
        v
cached TypeSafe capability and research-move index
        |
        v
hybrid retrieval: semantic + lexical + rare-feature + failure reservoir
        |
        v
TypeSafe pair audit: relation + move + bridge + correspondence
        |
        v
reasoning agent reads exact sources and constructs candidates
        |
        v
claim-appropriate exposing oracle and review
        |
        v
new accepted construction or typed failure node
```

Known authority, explicit dependencies, hashes, current-release membership,
issue state, arithmetic, graph traversal, and final composition stay in code.
TypeSafe supplies semantic features and pair judgments where exact code cannot
recognize that two constructions play similar roles.

The full accepted release and all 47 current `euler*.py` modules are indexed,
rather than only sources that share words with the active campaign. Module
cards are extracted deterministically from their module contract, public
signatures and docstrings, source hash, and matching test names. They remain
`provisional_code_module` nodes even when tested; only separately accepted
claims carry scientific authority. A new obligation is cheap relative to
rebuilding the index: it needs one obligation profile and a bounded set of
isolated pair requests. Each pair receives only one source and one obligation,
preventing the shared-state order contamination measured earlier.

## Required obstruction card

A useful stuck-mode call must contain an exact research state:

```yaml
parent_objective: the unchanged positive object
dependency_obligation: the proposition currently needed by a consumer
operator_and_equations: the exact load-bearing mathematical structure
admissible_space: topology, regularity, boundaries, ensemble, and representation
current_inputs: accepted or explicitly provisional constructions available now
failed_attempt: the representation or ansatz actually tried
failure_mechanism: the exact implication, determinant, mode, invariant, or scope that failed
preserved_constraints: user-owned invariants and accepted premises
exclusions: conclusions and imports that are not licensed
desired_handoff: construction, proof route, falsifier, observable, or source map
```

The generic statement “the campaign is stuck” was retained as a negative
control. It produced attractive but domain-arbitrary sources. TypeSafe became
useful only after the operator, admissible space, exact obstruction, and
consumer were present.

## Iterative method findings

The first portfolio experiment reduced the work to route ranking. That was the
wrong objective. It could compare candidates but could not teach the next
approach or make the rest of the framework available as research memory.

The method experiment then found two technical failures:

- shared-state order reversal changed one judgment by as much as `0.79` and
  the mean absolute judgment by `0.086`;
- adding irrelevant green-check rhetoric moved a structured evidence score by
  `1.10` on a four-point scale, despite instructions to ignore it.

The compass therefore strips surface tallies, determines authority in code,
and evaluates one semantic node per request.

The first full-registry index used broad features such as composition and scope
repair. They saturated, and the same P251 sources dominated unrelated
obligations. The repair was failure-driven feature discovery:

- separate content capabilities from research operations;
- use a `Choice` distribution for the single most load-bearing capability and
  the most relevant research operation;
- add rare coordinates for localized objects, internal basis/mixing/chirality,
  and nonlinear PDE matching;
- distinguish Hilbert/Fock/Born/exchange structure from ordinary probability
  weights;
- use feature-rarity weighting and reciprocal-rank fusion across independent
  retrieval channels; and
- reserve a small failure-mechanism reservoir so a relevance prefilter cannot
  hide an invertible failure.

The first pair audit still rewarded generic analogies. The final pair schema
added two independent questions: whether the exact structures correspond, and
whether the cards imply a source-specific research instruction. Deterministic
composition also checks whether the source actually supports the obligation's
dominant capability and research move.

The last correction addressed a repository-coverage failure. The accepted
release alone omitted current P253 constructions that already implement parts
of the proposed routes. The final index therefore adds the provisional code
layer and a graph-seed retrieval channel. In this prototype, the five seeds per
obligation came from GitNexus concept/context inspection plus imports and
matching tests. Future runs should populate that channel dynamically from the
current call graph, imports, tests, and active proposal before TypeSafe reranks
the candidates.

Calibration after these repairs is directionally correct on deliberately
contrasting records:

| --- | ---: |
| `C-VOP-001` supplies declared quantum state/measurement structure | 0.89 |
| classical `C-CST-014` mixture supplies quantum structure | 0.04 |
| `C-MIX-002` supplies internal-basis/mixing structure | 0.66 |
| `C-U1-001` supplies a charge/current construction | 0.81 |
| `C-RMAP-003` supplies chart-local Hessian/coercivity structure | 0.60 |

The obligation profiles also separate the active problems:

| Obligation | Dominant capability | Probability | Dominant move | Probability |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| actual partition-2 carrier | nonlinear PDE matching | 0.84 | assumption/scope repair | 0.56 |

These probabilities describe the supplied cards and question schema. They are
calibration evidence for this workflow, not universal thresholds.

## Source-informed approach changes for P253

### Actual partition-2 carrier

TypeSafe identifies the problem primarily as nonlinear PDE matching, with the
0161 regular-trace and `F'(zeta)=0` failures as the most structurally grounded
inversions. This changes the route from “insert a separatrix layer” to an
inverse matching construction:

1. locate an actual Cao-carrier `zeta` critical point with indefinite Hessian;
2. freeze its local hyperbolic orbit and KKS orientation as required target
   data;
3. construct the inner centralizer/separatrix and outer Euler correction
   together, retaining pressure, exterior velocity, circulation, impulse,
   phase, and coadjoint rows;
4. project the matching residual onto the continuum distorted adjoint, using
   the physical residue rather than the current two-mode witness; and
5. choose nonlinear amplitude only after the operator and boundary maps close.

`C-CST-011` supplies the useful construction order: fixed actual Euler carrier,
full pressure and tails, material/KKS rows, controlled finite window, analytic
applicability, and amplitude chosen last. Its packet is not the required
stationary Cao branch, so the missing applicability theorem is substantive.
The transfer is a method for organizing the actual construction, not evidence
that the branch exists.

Current code sharpens this further. `euler_axisymmetric_action` already retains
the axisymmetric canonical action, KKS form, boundary one-form, and endpoint
term, so another local canonical derivation is unlikely to resolve the
obligation. `euler_cao_schur` already supplies the leading physical
parameter/moment Jacobian and Schur determinant. The unfilled bridge is the
global chart/end matching and the Green/Fredholm reconstruction of an actual
branch. That directs the next construction toward the full boundary operator
and its physical adjoint rows, rather than another finite algebraic witness.

This creates a concrete exposing fork. Absence of an admissible indefinite
critical point, failure of the full boundary/invariant matching determinant,
or a zero/undefined continuum residue closes the smooth branch at its exact
mechanism. A successful solve supplies the actual stationary/relative-
equilibrium reconstruction still missing from the prepared-history analogies.

### Continuum trace and physical residue

The strongest source-specific transfer is `C-CST-012`. It controls an actual
Euler slow limit without a transport-frequency gap by selecting an isotypic
sector, retaining the translation kernel, and using conserved adjoint rows
with full pressure. `C-KRN-002` adds a distinct operation: subtract or match the
singular reference before taking a critical limit. `C-RES-001` supplies the
finite Schur/Feshbach algebra while explicitly fencing phenomenological loss.

Together they suggest this operator construction:

1. split the physical Euler block into the resonant row space and its
   complement, retaining both Hodge polarizations and finite
   circulation/interface/exterior rows;
2. form the source-sandwiched Grushin/Schur operator before the
   limiting-absorption limit;
3. isolate and reference-subtract the universal singular part before taking
   `epsilon -> 0`, rather than extrapolating an unsubtracted finite-mode trace;
4. obtain complement bounds from the exact symmetry/isotypic sector and
   conserved adjoint rows instead of assuming a spectral gap; and
5. prove uniform remainder, adjoint residual, units, delta scaling, and
   nonzero residue on the fixed actual carrier.

This directly targets the model-to-continuum bridge rather than adding another
finite matrix identity.

The provisional modules agree with that diagnosis while fencing its scope.
`euler_retained_memory` gives an exact finite bounded projection-memory
identity, and `euler_asymptotic_tails` supplies controlled tail machinery, but
neither proves the infinite Euler generator or the physical limiting-
absorption statement. Their correct use is inside the source-sandwiched
Grushin construction above, where the continuum remainder and residue can be
proved on the actual carrier.

### Quantum and statistics bridge

`C-VOP-001` is most useful as a contrastive construction. It has a normalized
coherent state, Weyl representation, and Born number law, while explicitly
stating that none of these quantizes or prepares the classical medium.
`C-OSC-001` adds the finite-truncation commutator defect. The continuous-action
and detector-reset failures identify two independent missing bridges.

The source-informed approach is to invert those exclusions:

1. compute the actual reduced Euler carrier's KKS cohomology and symplectic
   period lattice;
2. test whether a prequantum line bundle can exist through
   `integral Omega/(2*pi*hbar) in Z` on every physical compact two-cycle;
3. determine whether that condition fixes a nonzero action unit or remains
   continuously rescalable under allowed Euler similarities;
4. only after an integral physical orbit exists, construct the infinite Weyl
   representation and an operator map from physical carrier coordinates to
   `alpha`, preserving the full commutator rather than a finite truncation;
5. construct preparation, interaction, and detector capture/reset separately,
   then test whether a Born rule is derived or has been added as a postulate;
   and
6. derive exchange holonomy on the physical multi-carrier configuration
   space, rather than importing the conditional `C-SG-020` phase.

This route can produce either a real bridge or a sharp result that bare Euler
has no intrinsic prequantization scale at the selected orbit. Both outcomes
inform the next representation; neither is licensed by formal oscillator
algebra alone.

`euler_quantum_bridge` shows that the conditional algebra is already reusable:
it implements KKS-sphere prequantization, Euler action scaling, and conditional
exchange characters. `euler_gauge_action_selection` separately exposes a
dimensionful electromagnetic action scale while declining to derive the law
that selects it. The next approach should therefore construct the *physical*
compact Euler orbit, test its period lattice, and derive or refute scale
selection and detector dynamics. Reimplementing prequantization formulas would
leave the real bridge untouched.

### Euler-native charge or conversion process

TypeSafe identifies the missing variational law before the missing gauge law.
`C-CST-009` is the strongest construction source because it begins with one
pulled-back Euler material action, retains reaction momenta, eliminates them
only after variation, and exposes its finite-family reconstruction residual.
`C-CST-017` shows how a physical current can require improvement terms,
boundary flux, and memory while leaving total momentum unchanged.

The allotropic idea should therefore start with an inverse variational
construction:

1. identify two actual Euler relative equilibria on a common invariant leaf;
2. construct a physical reaction coordinate and its conjugate momentum inside
   one Euler material action;
3. derive the balance law, current, boundary flux, and any source term before
   naming the integrated conversion rate a charge;
4. test whether Euler material advection makes conversion identically zero or
   reduces it to boundary transport; and
5. if a nontrivial internal action survives, ask separately whether global
   topology or prequantization makes the resulting charge discrete and whether
   a derived sourced field couples to it.

The key scope test is whether the reaction coordinate is realized by
unrestricted Euler dynamics. A declared double-well free energy or conversion
law would be a new constitutive postulate unless this bridge is constructed.

The current code already contains conditional scale, current, and obstruction
pieces in `euler_gauge_action_selection`, `euler_ertel_current`,
`euler_two_label_lock`, and `euler_charged_hessian`. They make the missing
object more precise: a bare-Euler dynamical realization of the internal action
and conversion coordinate, with its boundary terms. Another formally conserved
or dimensionally correct current does not supply that realization.

### Neutrino mixing

TypeSafe correctly calls for an internal-basis construction composed with
explicit glue, but it also exposes a valuable false friend. `C-CST-014` has two
actual Euler column families with common frequency and a positive whole-
realization mixture. The claim explicitly says that this is a mixture, not a
stationary-field superposition. It therefore cannot produce coherent flavor
oscillation.

The approach should require:

1. two or three neutral modes on one physical background and one symplectic
   phase space, with a coherent amplitude vector rather than ensemble weights;
2. a propagation generator whose eigenbasis and eigenphase differences are
   physically normalized;
3. a distinct interaction or detection operator that defines the observable
   basis;
4. the relative unitary map between those two physically constructed bases;
5. only then, application of `C-MIX-002` to quotient rephasings and identify
   which angles or complex phases are invariant; and
6. a separate chiral/helicity and relativistic propagation bridge.

`C-CST-014` remains useful as a search specification: replace its positive
mixture of separate realizations by same-background coherent modes while
retaining actual Euler suppliers, common frequency control, complete energy,
and physical observation rows. If that same-background coherent construction
cannot exist, the mixture cannot be relabeled as neutrino oscillation.

`euler_neutrino_suppliers` already implements the exact two-flavor transition
formula, packet averaging, relativistic phase, material-frame Hamiltonian, and
helicity/current parity as a supplier ledger. The open work is consequently
the physical same-background modes, basis map, phase normalization, and
coupling to preparation and observation. Re-deriving the oscillation formula
would add no closure.

## Future campaign protocol

1. **Refresh authority and live construction state in code.** Read the pinned
   release, accepted claims, active proposal state, exact latest attempt,
   current reusable modules and tests, and explicit dependencies. Keep
   accepted and provisional nodes distinct.
2. **Write the obstruction card.** Include equations, operator, admissible
   space, consumer, failure, and scope fences.
3. **Reuse the cached index.** Recompute a source vector only when its exact
   claim hash, feature schema, or TypeSafe model changes.
4. **Retrieve for breadth.** Fuse semantic features, lexical search, rare
   capability channels, a bounded failure reservoir, and graph seeds from
   current callers/imports/tests. Preserve source and sector diversity.
5. **Adjudicate isolated pairs.** Ask relation, research move, missing bridge,
   approach information, novelty, scope conflict, structural correspondence,
   and whether a source-specific instruction exists.
6. **Audit exact sources.** The reasoning agent rederives the load-bearing
   implication and identifies the actual consumer. High TypeSafe probability
   does not waive applicability.
7. **Generate materially different constructions.** For an open mechanism,
   use the retrieved operations and sources to create at least two candidates
   under frozen criteria.
8. **Run the exposing oracle.** Prove, calculate, simulate, or falsify the
   exact proposition at its proper scope.
9. **Feed back the edge.** Record whether the source was consumed, required a
   repair, changed representation, supplied an oracle, conflicted, or was a
   distractor. Use those labels to discover or revise future TypeSafe features.

The feedback step is where the method can improve across campaigns. The
TypeSafe feature-discovery cookbook uses model errors to propose better
features; here, the labels should come from whether an independently reviewed
source transfer changed a real construction or exposed its failure. Route
counts and TypeSafe agreement are not suitable labels.

## Receipts and cost

The implementation is `run_research_compass.py`. It indexed all 271 claims in
the pinned `v0.183.0` release, 47 provisional Euler code modules, five frontier
failure nodes, and six obligation cards with `jev-1.13.0`. The final snapshot
contains 120 isolated pair edges.

- final snapshot inputs: 1,744,630 tokens;
- final snapshot outputs: 194,860 tokens;
- all iterative experiment inputs: 3,643,075 tokens;
- all iterative experiment outputs: 414,338 tokens; and
- typical incremental cost after caching the release index: about 62,000 to
  66,000 input tokens and about 9,100 output tokens for one obligation with 20
  pair audits.

Raw responses are append-only in `research-compass-responses.jsonl`; the current
composed graph is `research-compass.json`; the human-readable edge report is
`research-compass-report.md`. No credential is stored in these artifacts.
